WORKPLACE COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES

1. GENERAL COMMUNICATION

Employees should communicate with colleagues and managers respectfully and professionally.

2. DEADLINES

If an employee expects a delay, they should inform the responsible manager as early as possible and explain the reason for the delay.

3. MEETINGS

Important decisions made during meetings should be documented.

Action items should include the responsible person and expected deadline.

4. MANAGER COMMUNICATION

Employees should communicate potential project risks to their manager before the deadline becomes critical.

5. CROSS-CULTURAL COMMUNICATION

Employees working with international teams should consider differences in communication style, hierarchy, formality, and workplace etiquette.

6. CONFLICTS

Employees should avoid personal or confrontational language when discussing workplace disagreements.

Concerns should be communicated by focusing on the work, process, or situation rather than attacking an individual.

7. SENSITIVE INFORMATION

Employees should not share confidential company information with unauthorized people or external systems.
